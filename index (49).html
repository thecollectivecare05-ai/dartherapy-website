What to Expect

Your first 30 days with DARTherapy

Starting ABA can feel like a big step. Here's exactly what the journey looks like — clear, supportive and at your family's pace.

Start with a free consultation
Step by step

The first month, mapped out

Day 1 — Free consultation

We listen to your concerns, learn about your child, answer your questions and explain how ABA can help. No cost, no pressure.

Week 1 — Insurance & assessment

We verify your insurance benefits and your BCBA begins a Functional Behavior Assessment (FBA) to understand your child's strengths and needs.

Week 2 — Your individualized plan

Your BCBA translates the assessment into clear, measurable goals and a treatment plan built around your child and family. We review it together.

Weeks 3–4 — Therapy begins

One-to-one sessions start — at home, school or in the community — delivered by a trained technician and supervised by your BCBA.

Ongoing — Progress & reviews

We track data every session and meet with you regularly to review progress, celebrate wins and adjust goals as your child grows.

How to prepare

You don't need to do much

  • Have your insurance information handy (we'll do the rest)
  • Note the concerns and goals most important to your family
  • Any prior evaluations or a diagnosis report, if you have them
  • Just be yourself — this is a conversation, not a test

We move at your pace

Every family is different. Whether you're just exploring or ready to begin, we'll meet you where you are and never pressure you. The first consultation is simply a chance to talk.
