What it is

ABA Assessment & FBA with DARTherapy

A Functional Behavior Assessment looks beyond the surface of a behavior to understand why it happens — what need it meets and what maintains it. That understanding is what makes an ABA plan genuinely effective rather than one-size-fits-all.

During the assessment, our BCBA gathers information through observation, interviews and data, then translates it into clear, measurable goals and a treatment plan designed around your child's strengths and your family's priorities.

Questions

ABA Assessment & FBA, answered

What is a Functional Behavior Assessment (FBA)? +
An FBA is a structured process a BCBA uses to understand why a behavior occurs — what triggers it and what maintains it — through observation, interviews and data. The findings shape an individualized, effective treatment plan.
What happens after the assessment? +
Your BCBA translates the findings into clear, measurable goals and a treatment plan built around your child, then reviews it with you before therapy begins.
What ages do you assess? +
We work with children and teens ages 2 to 18. A free consultation is the first step.
Do you accept insurance? +
We work with the major plans that cover ABA in Florida, plus private pay, and verify benefits before starting.
Is the assessment available in Spanish? +
Yes — assessments and all family communication are available in English and Spanish.
