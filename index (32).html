Frequently Asked Questions

Your questions about ABA, answered

Everything families commonly ask about ABA therapy, insurance and getting started with DARTherapy. Still have a question? We're one call away.

Ask us anything
What is ABA therapy? +
Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) is an evidence-based therapy that helps children build communication, social, learning and daily-living skills, and reduce behaviors that get in the way. Every DARTherapy program is individualized and supervised by a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA).
What ages do you work with? +
We work with children and teens ages 2 to 18 across South Florida.
Where do you provide therapy? +
We offer in-home, school-based and community-based ABA across Broward, Miami-Dade and Palm Beach counties.
Do you offer services in Spanish? +
Yes. DARTherapy is a fully bilingual practice led by a bilingual BCBA. We serve families in English and Spanish.
Which insurance plans do you accept? +
We accept the major plans covering ABA in Florida — Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross Blue Shield (BCBS) and Tricare — plus private pay. We verify your benefits before beginning.
Do you accept Florida Medicaid? +
We are not currently an enrolled Florida Medicaid provider. If you have Medicaid, the Florida AHCA directory can help you find participating providers.
How do we get started? +
It starts with a free consultation. We talk through your child's needs and goals, verify your insurance, complete an assessment (FBA), and build an individualized plan before therapy begins.
What is a Functional Behavior Assessment (FBA)? +
An FBA is how a BCBA understands why a behavior happens — what triggers and maintains it — so the treatment plan addresses the real cause. It's the foundation of an effective, individualized program.
What's the difference between in-home, school and community ABA? +
In-home ABA happens in your home; school-based ABA supports your child in the classroom; community-based ABA builds skills in real-world settings like stores and parks. Many children benefit from a combination.
Do parents need to be involved? +
Family involvement is central to our approach — 'when you support the parent, you support the child.' We offer dedicated parent training so you can support progress every day.
How long does ABA therapy take? +
Every child is different. Goals, hours and duration are individualized based on the assessment and reviewed regularly. We'll give you a clear picture during the planning stage.

Ready to take the first step?

Book a free, no-pressure consultation with our bilingual team. We'll listen, answer your questions and help you understand the next step.
