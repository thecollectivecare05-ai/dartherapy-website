Our founder

Daniel A. Rivas, BCBA

Daniel is a bilingual, Florida-native Board Certified Behavior Analyst and the founder of DARTherapy. His path into behavior analysis began in healthcare — working as a physical therapy and recreational therapy aide in South Florida hospitals — before becoming a Certified Therapeutic Recreation Specialist (CTRS) in 2016.

He went on to join the Cadenza Center as a Registered Behavior Technician in 2018, working across schools, clinics, the community and family homes. After passing his board exam in 2020, he became a BCBA and served families for several years before founding DARTherapy — a practice built around the belief that family involvement is the key to meaningful, lasting progress.

Daniel holds a Bachelor's in Recreational & Sports Management from Florida International University and a Master's in Applied Behavior Analysis from Ball State University. He is currently pursuing a PhD in Applied Behavior Analysis, with research focused on family-included therapy.
