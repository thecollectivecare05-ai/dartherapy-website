Our services

In-Home ABA Therapy

Compassionate, one-to-one Applied Behavior Analysis delivered in your own home and guided by a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) — building real skills in the place your child lives, plays and learns.

School-Based ABA Therapy

School-based ABA brings individualized behavioral support into the learning environment — helping your child build the attention, communication and social skills that make the school day a success.

Learn more →

Community-Based ABA

Community-based ABA takes therapy beyond the home — to the store, the park, the restaurant and other everyday places — so your child can practice the skills that make community life easier for the whole family.

Parent Training

Parent training gives you the practical, evidence-based strategies to support your child's progress in everyday moments — turning you into your child's most powerful source of consistency.

Questions

ABA therapy, answered

What is ABA therapy? +
Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) is an evidence-based therapy that helps children build communication, social, learning and daily-living skills, and reduce behaviors that get in the way. At DARTherapy, every program is individualized and supervised by a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA).
What ages do you serve? +
We work with children and teens ages 2 to 18 across South Florida.
Where do you provide therapy? +
We offer in-home, school-based and community-based ABA across Broward, Miami-Dade and Palm Beach counties.
Do you speak Spanish? +
Yes. DARTherapy is a bilingual practice led by a bilingual BCBA, serving families fully in English and Spanish.
Do you accept insurance? +
We accept the major insurance plans that cover ABA in Florida, and private pay is available. We verify your benefits before anything begins.

Ready to help your child thrive?

Book a free, no-pressure consultation with our bilingual team. We'll listen, answer your questions and help you understand the next step.
