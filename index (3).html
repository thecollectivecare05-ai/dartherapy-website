Community-Based ABA

Building skills in the real world, one outing at a time

Community-based ABA takes therapy beyond the home — to the store, the park, the restaurant and other everyday places — so your child can practice the skills that make community life easier for the whole family.

What it is

Community-Based ABA with DARTherapy

Many of the moments that matter most happen out in the community: waiting in line, ordering food, crossing a parking lot safely, coping with noise and crowds. Community-based ABA deliberately practices these situations with your child, with a technician and BCBA supporting them in real time.

By teaching skills in the places they'll actually be used, we help your child build confidence and independence that transfers to daily life.

Why families choose it

The benefits

Real-world practice

Skills are built in authentic settings — stores, parks, restaurants — where they truly matter.

Safety awareness

We work on crucial safety skills like staying close, waiting and navigating busy spaces.

Social confidence

Ordering, greeting and interacting with others are practiced in natural situations.

Tolerance & flexibility

We gently build tolerance for waiting, noise, change and new environments.

Easier family outings

As skills grow, everyday outings become more manageable and enjoyable for everyone.

Compassionate, ethical care

Every outing is planned around your child's comfort, dignity and genuine progress.

How it works

Getting started is simple

From your first call to ongoing sessions, here's what the journey looks like.

Free consultation

We talk through your concerns, your child's needs and your goals — no cost, no pressure.

Assessment (FBA)

A BCBA completes a functional behavior assessment to understand how best to help.

Individualized plan

We design a treatment plan built around your child's strengths and your family's priorities.

Therapy sessions

One-to-one therapy begins, delivered by a technician and supervised by your BCBA.

Progress reviews

We track data continuously and meet with you regularly to review progress and adjust goals.

Is it right for your family?

Who it helps

  • Children who find community settings (stores, parks) challenging
  • Goals around safety, waiting and coping with busy environments
  • Building confidence for everyday family outings
  • Practicing social skills in natural, real-world situations

Questions

Community-Based ABA, answered

What is community-based ABA? +
Community-based ABA delivers therapy in real-world community settings — such as stores, parks and restaurants — so your child can practice safety, social and coping skills where they're actually needed, with BCBA-supervised support.
Is it safe for my child? +
Safety is central. Outings are carefully planned around your child's current skills, and building safety awareness is often one of the first goals.
What ages do you work with? +
We support children and teens ages 2 to 18. A free consultation helps us plan the right approach.
Do you accept insurance? +
We work with the major insurance plans covering ABA in Florida, plus private pay, and verify your benefits before starting.
Do you offer services in Spanish? +
Yes — we are a fully bilingual practice serving families in English and Spanish.
