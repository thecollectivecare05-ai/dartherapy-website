What it is

Parent Training with DARTherapy

Children spend far more time with their families than with any therapist. That's why parent training is one of the highest-impact parts of ABA: when caregivers understand and apply the same strategies, progress happens faster and lasts longer.

Our BCBA works with you directly — in plain language, at your pace — to build confidence around communication, routines, managing challenging moments and encouraging the behaviors you want to see. It's collaborative, judgment-free and tailored to your family.

Why families choose it

The benefits

Practical, usable strategies

You learn concrete techniques you can apply in real, everyday situations at home.

Better communication

Support your child's communication and reduce frustration for the whole family.

Consistency that compounds

Shared strategies between home and therapy make progress faster and more durable.

Confidence & less stress

Understanding the 'why' behind behavior helps you respond calmly and effectively.

Skills that last

The strategies you learn keep working long after any single therapy block ends.

Tailored to your family

Training is built around your child, your routines and your priorities.

How it works

Getting started is simple

From your first call to ongoing sessions, here's what the journey looks like.

Free consultation

We talk through your concerns, your child's needs and your goals — no cost, no pressure.

Assessment (FBA)

A BCBA completes a functional behavior assessment to understand how best to help.

Individualized plan

We design a treatment plan built around your child's strengths and your family's priorities.

Therapy sessions

One-to-one therapy begins, delivered by a technician and supervised by your BCBA.

Progress reviews

We track data continuously and meet with you regularly to review progress and adjust goals.

Questions

Parent Training, answered

What is ABA parent training? +
Parent training teaches caregivers the same evidence-based ABA strategies used in therapy, so you can support your child's communication, routines and behavior in everyday life — guided directly by a BCBA.
Do I need to have other ABA services to get parent training? +
Not necessarily. Parent training can complement your child's therapy or, in some cases, stand on its own. A free consultation helps us recommend the right fit.
How is training delivered? +
In plain language and at your pace, built around your family's real routines and priorities — collaborative and judgment-free.
Do you accept insurance? +
We work with the major plans covering ABA in Florida, plus private pay, and confirm coverage before starting.
Do you offer training in Spanish? +
Yes — we provide parent training and all communication in both English and Spanish.
