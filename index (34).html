A note on Medicaid

If you have Florida Medicaid

We want to be transparent: DARTherapy is not currently an enrolled Florida Medicaid provider, so we're unable to bill Medicaid or Medicaid managed-care plans. This isn't meant to exclude anyone — it's accurate information that saves families time.

If you have Medicaid, the Florida AHCA provider directory can help you find participating ABA providers in your area.

Private pay welcome

Prefer not to use insurance, or between plans? Private-pay arrangements are available. We'll walk you through the options clearly and help you choose what works for your family.

Questions

Insurance & funding, answered

Which insurance plans do you accept? +
We accept the major plans that cover ABA therapy in Florida, including Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross Blue Shield (BCBS) and Tricare. Private-pay options are also available. We'll confirm your specific plan during your consultation.
Does insurance cover ABA therapy in Florida? +
Florida state law requires most insurers to cover ABA therapy for children diagnosed with autism. Coverage details vary by plan, which is why we verify your benefits before anything begins.
What if my plan isn't listed? +
Reach out anyway — plans and networks change, and we're happy to check your specific coverage. If we can't bill your plan, we'll explain private-pay options clearly.
Do you accept Florida Medicaid? +
We are not currently an enrolled Florida Medicaid provider. If you have Medicaid, the Florida AHCA provider directory can help you find participating ABA providers in your area.
How does payment work? +
After we verify your benefits, we explain exactly what your plan covers and what, if anything, you're responsible for — clearly and up front, before therapy begins.
