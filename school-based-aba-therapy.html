What it is

School-Based ABA Therapy with DARTherapy

School is where children spend much of their day, and it brings its own demands: following routines, waiting, transitioning between activities, and interacting with peers. School-based ABA places a trained technician alongside your child in that setting, under BCBA supervision, to support those exact skills in the moment they're needed.

We coordinate closely with teachers and staff so that strategies are consistent across the classroom and the rest of your child's day, making progress more durable.

Why families choose it

The benefits

Support in the real setting

Skills are taught where they're used — during lessons, transitions and recess — so they generalize naturally.

Stronger peer interaction

We target the social and communication skills that help your child connect with classmates.

Classroom readiness

Attention, following instructions, waiting and transitioning are built step by step.

Teacher collaboration

We align with school staff so strategies stay consistent across the whole day.

Greater independence

The goal is always fading support as your child grows more capable and confident.

Compassionate, ethical care

Every plan follows current best practice and respects your child's dignity and comfort.

How it works

Getting started is simple

From your first call to ongoing sessions, here's what the journey looks like.

Free consultation

We talk through your concerns, your child's needs and your goals — no cost, no pressure.

Assessment (FBA)

A BCBA completes a functional behavior assessment to understand how best to help.

Individualized plan

We design a treatment plan built around your child's strengths and your family's priorities.

Therapy sessions

One-to-one therapy begins, delivered by a technician and supervised by your BCBA.

Progress reviews

We track data continuously and meet with you regularly to review progress and adjust goals.

Questions

School-Based ABA Therapy, answered

What is school-based ABA therapy? +
School-based ABA brings a trained behavior technician into your child's classroom or school setting, under BCBA supervision, to support attention, communication, social and self-regulation skills during the actual school day.
Do you coordinate with my child's teachers? +
Yes. We work alongside teachers and school staff so strategies are consistent, and we communicate regularly about goals and progress.
What ages do you work with? +
We support children and teens ages 2 to 18. A free consultation helps us determine the right fit for your child.
Do you accept insurance? +
We work with the major insurance plans that cover ABA in Florida, plus private pay. We verify your benefits and explain coverage before starting.
Do you offer services in Spanish? +
Yes — we are a bilingual practice and provide services and family communication in English and Spanish.
