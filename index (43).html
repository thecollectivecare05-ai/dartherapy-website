Our Team

The people behind your child's progress

DARTherapy is led by a Board Certified Behavior Analyst and supported by a dedicated, bilingual team — all committed to compassionate, family-centered care.

Founder & Clinical Director

Daniel Rivas, BCBA

A bilingual, Florida-native Board Certified Behavior Analyst, Daniel founded DARTherapy on the belief that lasting progress happens when the whole family is involved. He holds a Master's in Applied Behavior Analysis, is pursuing a PhD focused on family-included therapy, and brings roughly a decade of experience across schools, clinics, the community and in-home settings.
