What it is

In-Home ABA Therapy with DARTherapy

In-home ABA therapy pairs your child with a trained behavior technician who works alongside them in familiar surroundings, under the close supervision of a BCBA. Because learning happens where daily life actually takes place — at the kitchen table, during playtime, through everyday routines — the skills your child builds are more likely to stick and carry over.

Every program is individually designed around your child's strengths, goals and your family's priorities. No two plans look the same, and progress is measured and reviewed continuously so therapy always moves in the right direction.

Why families choose it

The benefits

A familiar, comfortable space

Children engage and regulate better in surroundings they know, reducing anxiety and making each session more productive.

Real family involvement

Parents and caregivers are part of the process, learning strategies they can use long after each session ends.

Skills for everyday life

We target communication, daily routines and independence using the real objects and moments of your home.

Flexible scheduling

Sessions fit around your family's routine — no commute, no clinic waiting rooms, less disruption to your day.

Natural skill generalization

Because skills are taught where they'll be used, children are better able to apply them across people and places.

Compassionate, ethical care

Every plan follows current best practice and is centered on your child's dignity, comfort and genuine progress.

How it works

Getting started is simple

From your first call to ongoing sessions, here's what the journey looks like.

Free consultation

We talk through your concerns, your child's needs and your goals — no cost, no pressure.

Assessment (FBA)

A BCBA completes a functional behavior assessment to understand how best to help.

Individualized plan

We design a treatment plan built around your child's strengths and your family's priorities.

Therapy sessions

One-to-one therapy begins, delivered by a technician and supervised by your BCBA.

Progress reviews

We track data continuously and meet with you regularly to review progress and adjust goals.

Questions

In-Home ABA Therapy, answered

What is in-home ABA therapy? +
In-home ABA therapy is Applied Behavior Analysis delivered in your child's own home. A behavior technician works one-to-one with your child under the supervision of a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA), building communication, social and daily-living skills in the environment where your child spends the most time.
What ages do you work with? +
We work with children and teens ages 2 to 18. The best way to know if in-home ABA is the right fit is a free consultation, where we discuss your child's needs and goals.
Do you accept insurance? +
We work with the major insurance plans that cover ABA in Florida, as well as private-pay options. During your consultation we confirm your benefits and explain exactly what is covered before anything begins.
Do you offer services in Spanish? +
Yes. DARTherapy ABA is a bilingual practice led by a bilingual BCBA. We provide services and all family communication in both English and Spanish.
Which areas do you serve? +
We provide in-home ABA across South Florida, including Broward (Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Coral Springs), Miami-Dade and Palm Beach (Boca Raton, West Palm Beach).
